## 1

Question: When should a manufacturer report a problem with a device?

Outcome: answered

Answer: Manufacturers of devices made available on the Union market, other than investigational devices, shall report any serious incident involving devices made available on the Union market (except expected side-effects which are clearly documented in the product information and quantified in the technical documentation and subject to trend reporting) and any field safety corrective action in respect of devices made available on the Union market (including field safety corrective actions undertaken in a third country if the reason is not limited to that third country). Reports shall be submitted through the electronic system referred to in Article 92.

Question: What should a manufacturer do after its device is on the market?

Outcome: answered

Answer: After placing a device on the market, manufacturers must plan, establish, document, implement, maintain, and update a post-market surveillance system that is proportionate to the risk class and appropriate for the type of device as an integral part of their quality management system.

## 3

Question: What should a manufacturer do if it needs to recall a device?

Outcome: answered

Answer: Manufacturers who consider or have reason to believe that a device which they have placed on the market or put into service is not in conformity with the regulation must immediately take the necessary corrective action to bring that device into conformity, to withdraw it, or to recall it, as appropriate. They must inform the distributors of the device in question and, where applicable, the authorised representative and importers accordingly.
