Question: What should a manufacturer do if it needs to recall a device?

Outcome: answered

Answer: Manufacturers who consider or have reason to believe that a device they have placed on the market or put into service is not in conformity with the Regulation must immediately take the necessary corrective action to bring the device into conformity, to withdraw it, or to recall it, as appropriate. They must also inform the distributors of the device and, where applicable, the authorised representative and importers.

Answer: Where the device presents a serious risk, manufacturers must immediately inform the competent authorities of the Member States in which they made the device available and, where applicable, the notified body that issued a certificate for the device, of the non-compliance and any corrective action taken.

Answer: Furthermore, manufacturers of devices made available on the Union market must report any field safety corrective action (which includes recalls and withdrawals undertaken for technical or medical reasons to prevent or reduce the risk of a serious incident) through the electronic system referred to in Article 92. Except in cases of urgency in which the manufacturer needs to undertake the field safety corrective action immediately, the manufacturer must report the field safety corrective action without undue delay in advance of it being undertaken.

Answer: When a field safety corrective action is taken, the manufacturer must ensure that information about the action is brought without delay to the attention of users of the device by means of a field safety notice (FSN). The FSN must allow correct identification of the device (including relevant UDIs) and the manufacturer (including the SRN if issued), clearly explain the reasons for the action and associated risks without understating the level of risk, and indicate all actions to be taken by users. Except in cases of urgency, the draft FSN content must be submitted to the evaluating or coordinating competent authority for comments, and the FSN must be entered into the electronic system under Article 92 to be accessible to the public.
