Question: When should a manufacturer report a problem with a device?

Outcome: answered

Answer: Manufacturers shall report any serious incident immediately after they have established the causal relationship between that incident and their device or that such causal relationship is reasonably possible and not later than 15 days after they become aware of the incident.
